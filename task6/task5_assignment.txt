TASK 5 — PINN / neural state-space model for the Silverbox oscillator

The Silverbox public record is SNLS80mV.mat. Channel V1 is the input voltage u(t)
and channel V2 is the output voltage y(t). The sampling frequency is
fs = 610.35 Hz, so dt = 1/fs.

Use the 4096 samples in slice [42650:46746]. Subtract the mean of u on this
excerpt and the mean of y on this excerpt, and use the centered signals afterward.
The first 3072 samples are the training record and the last 1024 are the test record.

The physical Duffing form is:
    y_ddot + a y_dot + b y + c y^3 = g u(t).

For the neural model, let v = y_dot and use the state:
    y_dot = v,
    v_dot = a_theta(y, v, u),
where a_theta is a multilayer fully connected PyTorch network. Choose its depth,
width, activation, scaling, optimizer, learning rate, training steps, and training
rollout length.

Integrate the state with classical fourth-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) at step dt,
driven by the measured input u.

FINAL EVALUATION:
After the architecture weights are fixed, perform one free run of the whole
4096-sample excerpt. Start at the first sample with the measured y and a
finite-difference velocity. Integrate through all 3072 training samples and
continue with the SAME STATE into all 1024 test samples. On the test segment,
the rollout may read u but may NOT read measured y.

Compute:
    train RMSE on the 3072 training samples,
    test RMSE on the 1024 test samples.

The test RMSE is:
    sqrt((1/1024) * sum((y_hat_i - y_i)^2)).

A zero predictor is about 0.06 V test RMS, so smaller is better.

The solution must report the architecture/hyperparameters, state equation,
training method, both free-run RMSE values, and save a plot of measured versus
predicted output on the test segment.
